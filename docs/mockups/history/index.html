B · Three synced charts ★ recommended

Fees, Volume and TVL stacked on one shared time axis, each with its own $ axis from 0. One finger draws one line through all three and one card with that day's numbers (per tier when combined). Tap a chart's title to make it big. Laptop adds a day table on the right that follows your pointer.

C · Calendar + table

A month calendar where each day is a cell coloured by fees or volume (switch), a row per fee tier, and a monthly table. Hold a cell for that day's card. Compact, less chart-like: best for "which weekdays pay".

Laptop · 1440 px

iPhone · 390 px

Data honesty: hatched cells = not read · outlined cells with a pale core = estimated · filled cells = exact · empty outline = before the pool existed. Colour steps are fifths of the range's own days, so one big day can't wash out the rest.
